Terms & Conditions

1. Waste Integrity: All plastic items logged for collection must be clean, dry, and free of hazardous or bio-waste contaminants.


2. Incentive Settlement: Balances reflected in the Expected Wallet are pre-audited estimations. Actual spendable balances are realized only after scale measurement by an authorized operator.


3. Store Credit Redemption: Real Wallet credits can be redeemed exclusively within the ECOEARN product catalog for listed sustainable merchandise.

Privacy Policy

1. Location Data: GPS coordinates are accessed strictly with user consent to display genuine recycling aggregators via OpenStreetMap on the interactive map.


2. Transaction Records: User deposit histories, order receipts, and ratings are securely encrypted on Google Firebase Firestore.


3. Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or monetize personal customer details with commercial advertising networks.

Deposit & Coupon Standards

1. Premium Barcode 10% Discount: Premium members unlock barcode scanning to generate instant 10% discount vouchers redeemable across all sustainable products in our catalog.


2. Bulk Scrap Weight Standard: Bulk mixed dry plastic scrap is standardized at the base municipal rate of ₹20 per Kilogram.


3. Stacking Policy: Users can combine their 10% discount coupon codes with their verified Real Wallet credits at checkout for maximum savings.

OUR MISSION

Welcome to ECOEARN, India's premier digital circular economy platform dedicated to combating plastic pollution while rewarding community action. Our mission is simple yet impactful: to bridge the gap between waste generation and sustainable consumption by turning everyday plastic recyclables into valuable digital credits.

Based out of the coastal hub of Visakhapatnam, ECOEARN empowers citizens to directly reduce their municipal carbon footprint. Every kilogram of plastic waste you log into our smart calculator keeps debris away from pristine marine ecosystems and local landfills, converting your eco-conscious efforts into tradeable currency.

How It Works

  • Collect: Gather your plastic recyclables at home.
  • Calculate: Input the weight in KGs to view your reward rate (₹20/KG).
  • Earn: Instantly add the digital currency rewards straight to your wallet balance.
  • Shop: Spend your credits directly in our sustainable catalog of over 100+ plastic-free items.
