Help & Support

Q1: How do I convert Expected Wallet to Real Wallet?
Expected Wallet is an estimate based on your calculator or barcode entries. When an operator collects and weighs your recyclables, they will scan your Collector QR and credit your Real Wallet instantly.

Q2: How does the 25% Barcode Deposit work?
Scan or type the 13-digit barcode of eligible plastic containers, enter the retail MRP, and 25% of that price will be added to your Expected Wallet.

Q3: How do I schedule a home pickup?
Add items from the Eco-Catalog to your cart, proceed to checkout, and fill in your address and preferred collection date.

Terms & Conditions

1. Digital Incentive Settlement: Rewards displayed in the Expected Wallet are calculated estimates and do not constitute cash or spendable credit until physically audited by an authorized collector agent.


2. Waste Segregation Protocol: Only clean, dry, non-hazardous plastic recyclables are eligible for weighing and credit settlement.


3. Store Purchase & Delivery: Credits stored in the Real Wallet are redeemable strictly inside the ECOEARN sustainable catalog for listed eco-friendly goods.

OUR MISSION

Welcome to ECOEARN, India's premier digital circular economy platform dedicated to combating plastic pollution while rewarding community action. Our mission is simple yet impactful: to bridge the gap between waste generation and sustainable consumption by turning everyday plastic recyclables into valuable digital credits.

Based out of the coastal hub of Visakhapatnam, ECOEARN empowers citizens to directly reduce their municipal carbon footprint. Every kilogram of plastic waste you log into our smart calculator keeps debris away from pristine marine ecosystems and local landfills, converting your eco-conscious efforts into tradeable currency.

How It Works

  • Collect: Gather your plastic recyclables at home.
  • Calculate: Input the weight in KGs to view your reward rate (₹20/KG).
  • Earn: Instantly add the digital currency rewards straight to your wallet balance.
  • Shop: Spend your credits directly in our sustainable catalog of over 100+ plastic-free items.
